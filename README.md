## About me

I have been building mobile apps for more than five years. I started with native Android in Java and XML, moved to Kotlin and then Jetpack Compose, and today most of my work is in Flutter with BLoC and Riverpod. I have published apps on Google Play and the App Store, and when a project needs a backend or a web side, I build that too.

On the research side, I work on AI and signal processing methods that help detect heart disease earlier from heart sounds, ECG and cardiac images.

I am doing a master's in Computer Engineering at the Islamic University of Gaza (started 2026). Before that I earned a BSc in Mobile Computing and Smart Device Applications from the same university (2025).

## Featured projects

### Flutter

- [Blog App](https://github.com/mosabmoammar/flutter_blog_app): a blogging app on Supabase (auth, Postgres and storage), built with Clean Architecture and BLoC. Saved posts stay readable offline through a Hive cache.
- [Consultant Marketplace](https://github.com/mosabmoammar/consultant_customer_app): connects customers with consultants, with categories, a wallet, consultant availability and Arabic right-to-left layouts. Built with Riverpod; the UI flows are done and backend integration is in progress.
- [Budget Calculator](https://github.com/mosabmoammar/budget_app_flutter_lu): one codebase for Android, iOS and the web, with email and Google sign-in, per-user data in Cloud Firestore and state handled by hooks_riverpod.
- [The Network](https://github.com/mosabmoammar/graduation_project_the_network): my graduation project, built in a team of four. University students use it to chat in department groups, share study summaries and vote in polls. Flutter and Firebase.

### Android (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose)

- [News App](https://github.com/mosabmoammar/news-app): paged headlines and search from NewsAPI, with offline bookmarks in Room. Clean Architecture, MVVM and Hilt.
- [Stock Market App](https://github.com/mosabmoammar/stock-market): company listings cached in Room and intraday price charts drawn on a Compose Canvas, using Alpha Vantage data.
- Real-time chat: an Android [client](https://github.com/mosabmoammar/chat-app-ktor) and a Ktor [server](https://github.com/mosabmoammar/ktor-server-chat) that exchange messages over WebSockets and keep the history in MongoDB.

### Backend and other work

- [Ecomly Server](https://github.com/mosabmoammar/ecomly_server): a Node.js, Express and MongoDB REST API for an e-commerce app. It covers JWT auth, carts, Stripe checkout with webhooks and an admin API.
- [Pharma Visit System](https://github.com/mosabmoammar/oracle-apex-pharma-visit-system): a bilingual Oracle APEX and PL/SQL system for planning medical visits and dispensing drug samples. The business rules live in the database and are covered by tests; the APEX app is in progress.
- [Humans vs Orcs](https://github.com/mosabmoammar/hvo): a playable 2D real-time strategy prototype in Unity and C#, with A* pathfinding, resource gathering and waves of enemy attacks.

## Research and publications

I apply signal processing and machine learning to phonocardiogram (PCG), ECG and cardiac imaging data. The methods I use include multifractal detrended fluctuation analysis (MFDFA), DFA, spectral entropy and machine learning classifiers.
